# Tooling Philosophy

When building tools for content teams, **time-to-feedback** is the north star.

> If a workflow takes minutes to validate, teams stop experimenting.

## Principles

- Optimize for confidence and clarity
- Instrument usage early
- Automate repetitive setup paths
